# Effect textures

Shared by every cover that uses the effect, published from `src/themes/large-print/assets/images/motion/`. These are the lossless masters; `rain.webp` is published at 768x512, `drops.webp` at 512x512, `steam.webp` at 256x512.

## rain.webp

Made by Codex, with no attached image.

> Generate ONE image, 1536x1024 pixels: a layer of falling rain for a soft watercolour illustration. Pure black background (#000000). On it, many thin rain streaks in white and pale grey, all slanting the same way, from the upper right to the lower left at about 15 degrees from vertical, drawn by hand with a fine pen: short and long streaks, some fainter than others, evenly spread over the whole image with no empty band and no denser band, nothing else at all. No drops, no splashes, no clouds, no ground, no text. Save it as rain.png in the current directory.

## drops.webp

Made by Codex, with no attached image.

> Generate ONE image, 1024x1024 pixels: raindrops on a window pane, for a soft watercolour illustration. Pure black background (#000000). On it, about forty raindrops of different sizes, from tiny dots to drops about 30 pixels wide, drawn by hand in white and pale grey with a soft watercolour edge and a small bright highlight, a few of them with a short thin trail running straight down behind them. Evenly spread over the whole image, with no empty band and no denser band. Nothing else at all: no glass frame, no background, no text. Save it as drops.png in the current directory.

## steam.webp

Made by Codex, with no attached image.

> Generate ONE image, 512x1024 pixels, portrait: a single wisp of steam rising from a hot drink, for a soft watercolour illustration. Pure black background (#000000). The steam is drawn in white and pale grey with soft, loose watercolour edges: it starts thin and faint at the bottom centre, rises in two or three gentle curling strands that drift and widen, and fades away completely before the top. Nothing else at all: no cup, no background, no text. Save it as steam.png in the current directory.

## meteor.webp

Made by Codex, with the Halloween cover attached as the style reference, then trimmed. Packed into a cover's sprite atlas by the builder when its spec names `meteor`.

> Generate one image with your image generation tool and save it as meteor.png in the current directory, 1536x512, with a TRANSPARENT background (PNG with alpha). If transparency is impossible, use a flat pure black #000000 background with nothing else on it. Do not ask questions; decide.
>
> Subject: a single shooting star, horizontal, its bright head at the right end and its tail fading out to the left, filling most of the width. Hand painted in naive gouache like the attached picture's stars: a small cream and pale gold five-pointed star head with a soft glow, and a tail of a few loose dry brush strokes in cream #efe4c8 and pale gold #e6c77a that thin out and fade to nothing. Soft, calm, magical, not a laser beam, no sparkles all over. Nothing else in the picture: no sky colour, no other stars, no text.
